Privacy Policy - Animal Rencontre

Last updated: 14/07/2026

This privacy policy explains what data is collected in the Animal Rencontre application, how it is used and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

Application: Animal Rencontre
Contact: support.animalrencontre@gmail.com

2. Data collected

  • User account (Google sign-in, Firebase identifier)
  • Profile data (display name, age, city, preferences, chosen animal)
  • Messages and photos shared in chat
  • Technical information required for the app to work (push token)

3. Purposes

  • To enable sign-in and use of the application
  • To display profiles, matches and messaging
  • To send notifications for new messages
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service

4. Storage and processors

Data is stored via Firebase services (Google Cloud), including Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions.

5. Data retention

Data is kept while the account is active, then deleted within a reasonable period after a deletion request.

6. Your rights

You may request:

  • Access to your data
  • Correction of your data
  • Deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise these rights: support.animalrencontre@gmail.com

7. Account deletion

You may request complete deletion of your account and associated data by sending an email to support.animalrencontre@gmail.com with your account email address.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shall prevail.
