Child Safety Standards - Animal Rencontre

Last updated: 14/07/2026

Animal Rencontre maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and any content that endangers minors.

1. Adults only

This is a dating app for users aged 18 and over only. Registration requires a date of birth, and profiles belonging to minors are not allowed.

2. Prohibited content

  • Any sexually exploitative content involving minors
  • Profiles, messages, or photos that endanger a child
  • Grooming, harassment, or solicitation targeting minors
  • Any other violation of applicable child protection laws

3. In-app reporting

Users can report a profile, message, or suspicious behaviour from within the app using the « Report a safety issue » button in the user profile section.

You may also contact us directly at: support.animalrencontre@gmail.com

When reporting, please include if possible:

  • The username or profile involved
  • A description of the content or behaviour
  • The approximate date of the incident

4. Actions we take

When a report is received, we commit to:

  • Review the report within a reasonable timeframe
  • Suspend or remove accounts and content that violate these standards
  • Preserve relevant information when an investigation is required
  • Cooperate with competent authorities when required by law

5. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child protection laws and report illegal content or behaviour to the relevant authorities when required by regulation.

6. Safety contact

For any question regarding child safety and app compliance: support.animalrencontre@gmail.com
