Support - Animal Rencontre

Need help? We're here for you.

For any question, issue or request, contact us by email:
support.animalrencontre@gmail.com

We aim to reply within 48 hours (business days).

How can we help?

  • Sign-in issues (Google or email)
  • Questions about your profile, matches or messaging
  • Pause or resume your account
  • Delete your account and data
  • Report a profile, content or inappropriate behaviour
  • Report a bug or technical problem

Pause or delete your account

In the app, open your profile, then use the Pause my account button (you no longer appear in search) or Delete my account (permanent deletion of your account and data).

You can also request deletion by email at support.animalrencontre@gmail.com, mentioning your account email address.

Report a safety issue

To report a profile or content, email support.animalrencontre@gmail.com with the username involved and a description of the issue. We handle these reports as a priority.
